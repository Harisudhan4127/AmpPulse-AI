Abstract : 
 AmpPulse AI is an IoT and Artificial Intelligence-based smart energy monitoring and management system designed to provide a clear understanding of electricity consumption at the individual-appliance level. In homes, organizations, and industries, users generally know only their total electricity consumption from the monthly EB bill and cannot easily identify which appliances consume more power or cause unnecessary energy wastage. AmpPulse AI addresses this problem by continuously monitoring electrical appliances such as air conditioners, refrigerators, water heaters, washing machines, and other devices.

The proposed system uses current and voltage measurement sensors, an energy-metering module, and an ESP32-C3 microcontroller to collect real-time electrical parameters such as voltage, current, power, energy consumption, frequency, and operating status. The ESP32-C3 transmits the collected data through Wi-Fi to a cloud database for storage and processing. An AI-based analysis module studies real-time and historical consumption patterns to identify abnormal or excessive energy usage, estimate electricity costs, predict future consumption, and provide intelligent energy-saving recommendations. A web or mobile dashboard displays appliance-wise consumption, live status, estimated cost, usage history, and monthly reports.

AmpPulse AI can be extended for homes, organizations, industries, and government energy-management applications through separate dashboards and access levels. The system enables users and energy managers to understand where electricity is being consumed and take timely corrective actions. It also supports energy comparison, performance analysis, and long-term energy-efficiency planning.

By integrating **IoT, Cloud, AI, and real-time energy monitoring**, AmpPulse AI converts raw electricity-consumption data into meaningful insights. The system aims to reduce unnecessary energy wastage, lower electricity costs, improve energy efficiency, and promote smarter and more sustainable energy management.
